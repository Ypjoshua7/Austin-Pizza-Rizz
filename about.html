About the founder

Made with heart, one neighborhood pizza night at a time.

Austin Pizza Rizz began with a simple idea: create a pizza experience that feels like a warm welcome, not a rushed transaction. Our founder wanted to bring neighbors together around a fresh, homemade pie made with care.

From early pizza nights to local pickup orders, every recipe is inspired by the joy of sharing good food with the people around you. The goal is simple—serve honest pizza, genuine hospitality, and a little extra neighborhood love.

“Good pizza feels like home, and home should always be a place people want to come back to.”
